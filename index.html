Hello! Welcome to my website.

I am a Post Doctoral Research Scholar in the Department of Biostatistics, Johns Hopkins University. I obtained my PhD in Statistics from Texas A&M University, and my Master's degree from Indian Statistical Institute.

My postdoctoral advisor is Dr. Ni Zhao and my PhD advisor was Dr. Xianyang Zhang

My research interests include causal inference, high dimensional statistics and microbiome data. Recently I've branched out to developing methodologies for analysis of image based spatial microbiome data.
