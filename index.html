Hello! Welcome to my website.

I am a Postdoctoral Research Scholar in the Department of Biostatistics, Johns Hopkins University. I obtained my PhD in Statistics from Texas A&M University, and my Master's degree from Indian Statistical Institute.

My postdoctoral advisor is Dr. Ni Zhao and my PhD advisor was Dr. Xianyang Zhang. In the past I have worked on methodological problems related to causal inference, high dimensional statistics and microbiome abundance data.

Recently I've branched out to developing methodologies for analysis of image based spatial microbiome data. Emerging imaging technology allows us to record spatial arrangement of microbial taxa at a cellular level. My current work focuses on nonparamteric inference for cross-taxon dependence, and whether this dependence changes between healthy and diseased samples.
